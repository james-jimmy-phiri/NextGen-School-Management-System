# Mobile App Implementation Modules Roadmap

Please refer to [`mobile/ROADMAP.md`](../mobile/ROADMAP.md) for the detailed architectural breakdown and sprint milestones of the NextGen School Mobile Application.

### Quick Summary of Mobile Modules:
- **Module 1 (Completed)**: Foundation, Multi-Role Authentication, Sanctum Interceptors, Secure Storage & Role/Permission Routing Engine.
- **Module 2 (Completed)**: Parent Portal & Child 360 Experience (Multi-child switcher, Attendance, Marks & Continuous Assessments, Fees & Invoices, Timetable).
- **Module 3 (Completed)**: Teacher Workspace & Offline-First Attendance Register (One-tap roll call, Hive queue & sync, marks entry, class schedule).
- **Module 4 (Completed)**: Communications (Announcements, 1-on-1 Parent-Teacher Messaging, School Calendar & Events).
- **Module 5 (Completed)**: Mobile Finance & In-App Payments (Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, Digital Invoices & Official PDF/Voucher Receipts in MWK).
- **Module 6 (Completed)**: School Leadership & Executive Telemetry (Live Telemetry KPIs, Multi-category Approvals Workflow, Emergency SMS/Push Broadcast).
